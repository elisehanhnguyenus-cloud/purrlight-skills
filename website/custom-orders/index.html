How it works

  1. 1

    Send your photos. Face, full body, and any detail you want captured.

  2. 2

    Pay a 50% deposit. For every made-to-order or custom piece, we send you a design mockup before we start.

  3. 3

    We make it. We let you know when your piece is in the works, and can send you a progress update after about a week.

  4. 4

    Approve & receive. We send you a photo of the finished piece. Pay the balance, then it ships free in the U.S. — or pick it up at Pearland Town Center.
