chapter five · from Merlin’s notebook

Why “Purrlight”? Every stitch carries a memory.

Purrlight Studio began at a kitchen table in Pearland, Texas — one skein of yarn, one crochet hook, and one very real tuxedo cat named Merlin supervising from the windowsill. “Purr” is for the sound that fills our studio; “Light” is for the ones we’ve loved and lost, carrying the memory of our beloved husky who passed at five years old.

That is why we only make things meant to be kept. Today, we design every piece in Texas and craft them slowly with our family of artisan makers in Vietnam. Nothing mass-produced, nothing rushed — just soft heirlooms folded into memory boxes, not landfills.
