Every stitch has a story

Purrlight Studio began at a kitchen table in Pearland, Texas — one skein of yarn, one crochet hook, and a cat supervising from the windowsill.

Today we design every piece in Texas and craft them in small batches with our family of artisan makers in Vietnam. Nothing mass-produced, nothing rushed — just soft things made carefully, for people who notice the difference.

Made by hand

Crocheted, sewn, and finished by real hands — small batches, never a factory line.

Thoughtful materials

Soft cotton yarns and fabrics chosen to be gentle on small hands and curious paws.

Kept, not thrown away

Pieces made to become keepsakes — folded into memory boxes, not landfills.

Why “Purrlight”?

It’s the little glow a purring cat brings to a quiet room — the feeling we try to sew into everything we make. Small, warm, and impossible not to smile at.
