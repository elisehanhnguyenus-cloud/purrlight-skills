chapter five · two homes, one story

Every stitch has a story

Purrlight Studio began at a kitchen table in Pearland, Texas — one skein of yarn, one crochet hook, and one very real tuxedo cat named Merlin supervising from the windowsill.

Today we design every piece in Texas and craft them in small batches with our family of artisan makers in Vietnam. Nothing mass-produced, nothing rushed — just soft things made carefully, for people who notice the difference.

Why “Purrlight”?

“Purr” for the sound that fills our studio — Merlin’s, mostly. “Light” for the ones we’ve loved: the name also carries the memory of a husky we lost at five years old. It’s why this studio makes things that are meant to be kept.
