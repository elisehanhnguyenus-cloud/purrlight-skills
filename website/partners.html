The Purrlight Partner Program is for curators, creators, and gift-guide makers who love putting handmade things in front of the right people — and getting paid for it. One keepsake for one reader, or a small-batch run for a whole team.

Real commission

Earn a commission on every sale you send through TikTok Shop's affiliate program — set up in a few taps, tracked automatically, paid by TikTok.

Products people keep

Handmade dolls, baby keepsakes, and pet accessories with a story behind every stitch — the kind of finds gift guides are made of.

A partner, not a vendor

Content samples, early looks at new companions, and a real human answering your messages — usually the founder herself.

how it worksThree steps to your first commission

  1. Find us on TikTok Shop

    Open the TikTok Shop affiliate marketplace, search for Purrlight Studio, and add our products to your showcase. Commission rates are listed on each product.

  2. Create in your own voice

    Unboxings, gift guides, shelf styling, cat reactions — whatever your audience loves. We can share product details, story angles, and B-roll ideas if you want them.

  3. Get paid automatically

    TikTok tracks every sale from your content and pays your commission directly. No invoices, no spreadsheets, no chasing.
