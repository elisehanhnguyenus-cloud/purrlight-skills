Shipping & returns

Two ways to buy from us: pieces from our Etsy shop, and custom pieces you order directly on our Custom Orders page.

Shipping & processing

Pieces from our Etsy shop

Designed in Pearland, Texas · Handmade in Vietnam. Made-to-order pieces are crafted in small batches — current processing times are listed on each Etsy listing. Most pieces ship with tracking straight from our Vietnam workshop, duties and import fees already paid; a few ready-made pieces ship from our Texas studio. Delivery typically takes 10–14 days once a piece leaves the workshop, though customs can add time, especially toward the end of the year.

Custom Orders

Order direct · Free U.S. shipping, or pickup at Pearland Town Center (Sat & Sun). Each piece shows how soon it ships on the Custom Orders page.

Returns & exchanges

Pieces from our Etsy shop

Every piece is checked by hand before it ships. If something arrives less than perfect, message us on Etsy — we'll make it right. Each Etsy listing shows its return and exchange terms.

Custom Orders

Refunds: for an order we haven't started and materials we haven't bought, if you cancel at least 5 days before we start. Last-minute orders can't be refunded. Custom pieces can't be returned, but anything wrong or damaged is on us, 100%.
